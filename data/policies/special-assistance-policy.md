# ACME Bharat Airlines: Special Assistance Policy

## 1. Introduction
ACME Bharat Airlines is committed to providing a safe, comfortable, and dignified travel experience for all our guests. We recognize that some passengers require additional support during their journey. This Special Assistance Policy outlines the services, requirements, and procedures designed to accommodate passengers with diverse needs, including mobility limitations, young travelers, expectant mothers, and those requiring medical equipment.

## 2. Wheelchair Assistance
To ensure seamless mobility throughout the airport and during boarding, ACME Bharat Airlines provides comprehensive wheelchair assistance.
* **Charges:** This service is provided entirely free of charge to all passengers who require mobility support.
* **Pre-booking Requirement:** Passengers must request wheelchair assistance at least 48 hours before their scheduled flight departure. This notice allows our ground operations team to arrange the necessary personnel and equipment.
* **Service Scope:** Our assistance covers transfer from the departure terminal check-in counter to the boarding gate, boarding the aircraft, and transfer from the aircraft to the arrival terminal exit.

## 3. Unaccompanied Minors
ACME Bharat Airlines offers a dedicated service to ensure the safety and well-being of young travelers flying without an adult guardian.
* **Eligibility:** This service is mandatory for children aged between 5 and 12 years who are traveling alone.
* **Service Fee:** A service fee of Rs 3,000 per unaccompanied minor per flight sector is applicable.
* **Drop-off and Pick-up:** A parent or guardian must accompany the child to the departure check-in counter, complete the required indemnity forms, and remain at the airport until the flight has departed. At the destination, the child will only be released to the authorized guardian specified in the booking documents.

## 4. Pregnant Passengers
To ensure the health and safety of pregnant passengers and their unborn children, ACME Bharat Airlines has established specific travel guidelines.
* **Before 28 Weeks:** Expectant mothers with an uncomplicated pregnancy may travel without any medical clearance.
* **After 28 Weeks:** A valid "Fit-to-Fly" certificate is mandatory for any passenger who has reached or exceeded 28 weeks of pregnancy. 
* **Certificate Validity:** The certificate must be issued by a registered medical practitioner or obstetrician within 7 days of the scheduled departure date. It must clearly state the estimated date of delivery, confirm that the pregnancy is uncomplicated, and certify that the passenger is fit to travel by air.

## 5. Carriage of Medical Equipment
ACME Bharat Airlines facilitates the transport of essential personal medical equipment for passengers who require support during their journey.
* **Cost and Allowance:** Approved personal medical equipment is carried free of charge and does not count toward the passenger's standard checked or cabin baggage allowance.
* **Permitted Devices:** This policy applies to personal mobility aids, CPAP machines, portable oxygen concentrators (POC), and other certified life-support devices.
* **Prior Notification:** Passengers must inform the airline at least 48 hours before departure regarding the specifications of any medical equipment they intend to bring on board, particularly if the device requires battery power.
