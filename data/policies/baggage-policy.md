# ACME Bharat Airlines: Baggage Policy

This document outlines the official baggage regulations, allowances, fees, and claims procedures for passengers traveling with ACME Bharat Airlines. We are committed to ensuring a safe, efficient, and comfortable journey for all travelers. Please review these policies carefully prior to your departure.

## Cabin Baggage Allowance

To ensure the safety and comfort of everyone on board, cabin baggage must comply with strict size and weight restrictions. 

* **Quantity:** Each passenger is permitted to carry exactly one piece of cabin baggage.
* **Weight Limit:** The maximum weight allowed for the single piece of cabin baggage is 7 kg.
* **Personal Items:** Passengers may also carry one small personal item, such as a laptop bag, small handbag, or infant food bag, which must fit under the seat in front of them.

Any cabin baggage exceeding the 7 kg weight limit or the prescribed dimensions must be checked in at the airport counter and will be subject to applicable baggage policies.

## Checked Baggage Allowance

ACME Bharat Airlines offers a generous checked baggage allowance to facilitate a hassle-free travel experience.

* **Allowance:** The complimentary checked baggage allowance is 23 kg per passenger.
* **Applicability:** This standard allowance applies to all domestic flights operated by ACME Bharat Airlines.
* **Dimensions:** The sum of the 3 dimensions (length + width + height) of each checked bag must not exceed 158 cm (62 inches). 

For safety and operational reasons, any individual piece of baggage weighing more than 32 kg cannot be accepted as checked baggage and must be transported as cargo.

## Excess Baggage Charges

Passengers whose checked baggage exceeds the complimentary limit will be subject to excess baggage charges at the airport.

* **Rate:** Excess baggage will be charged at a flat rate of Rs 500 per kg.
* **Payment:** Charges must be paid in full at the airport check-in counter before a boarding pass can be issued. 
* **Pre-booking:** Passengers may pre-book additional baggage weight online through the ACME Bharat Airlines website or mobile app up to 2 hours before departure to access discounted rates.

## Delayed Baggage Procedures

In the rare event that your checked baggage does not arrive at your destination airport, ACME Bharat Airlines will make every effort to locate and deliver your baggage as quickly as possible.

* **Reporting Window:** Passengers must report the delayed baggage and file a Property Irregularity Report (PIR) at the airport baggage service desk within 4 hours of arrival.
* **Interim Compensation:** To cover the cost of essential items, ACME Bharat Airlines provides an interim compensation of Rs 1,000 per day.
* **Duration:** This interim compensation is payable for up to a maximum of 5 days, or until the baggage is delivered, whichever is earlier.

## Lost Baggage Claims

If ACME Bharat Airlines is unable to locate your checked baggage within a reasonable timeframe, the baggage will be officially declared lost.

* **Timeframe:** Baggage that remains untraced after 21 days from the date of flight arrival will be formally classified as lost.
* **Compensation:** Passengers are eligible to claim compensation up to Rs 20,000 for lost baggage. 
* **Claim Process:** To initiate a claim, passengers must submit the PIR, the original baggage tag, and an itemized list of the contents with supporting receipts.

## Damaged Baggage Claims

ACME Bharat Airlines takes utmost care in handling passenger baggage. However, if your baggage is damaged during transit, you are entitled to file a claim.

* **Reporting Procedure:** Passengers must file a damage report at the airport baggage service desk within 7 days of their arrival.
* **Required Documentation:** The claim must be accompanied by the original baggage tag and clear photos of the damage.
* **Exclusions:** ACME Bharat Airlines is not liable for minor wear and tear, scratches, scuffs, or damage resulting from over-packing or inherent defects in the luggage.
