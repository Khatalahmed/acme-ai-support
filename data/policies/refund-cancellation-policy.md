# ACME Bharat Airlines: Refund and Cancellation Policy

## 1. Introduction
This Refund and Cancellation Policy outlines the rules, procedures, and passenger rights concerning ticket cancellations, refunds, and flight disruptions for ACME Bharat Airlines. We are committed to providing transparent service and ensuring a fair process for all passengers. This policy applies to all tickets purchased directly through ACME Bharat Airlines' official channels, including our website, mobile application, and ticketing offices.

## 2. 24-Hour Risk-Free Cancellation Window
ACME Bharat Airlines offers a flexible cancellation window for passengers who need to alter their travel plans shortly after booking.
* Any cancellation made within 24 hours of booking qualifies for a full refund of the entire ticket price.
* This policy applies regardless of the fare type purchased (including non-refundable and promotional fares).
* No cancellation fees or penalties will be levied for transactions cancelled within this 24-hour window.

## 3. Policies by Fare Type
If a passenger cancels a booking outside of the initial 24-hour window, the refund eligibility depends on the category of fare purchased:

### Fully-Flexible Fares
* Passengers who purchase fully-flexible fares are entitled to a 100% refund of the ticket price upon cancellation.
* The refund will be processed and returned to the original form of payment within 5 business days from the date the cancellation request is submitted.

### Non-Refundable Fares
* Base fares and carrier-imposed charges for non-refundable tickets are non-reimbursable.
* However, all government-mandated taxes and airport fees included in the ticket price will be refunded to the passenger.
* Additionally, the remaining eligible portion of the fare will be returned in the form of a travel credit voucher. This voucher is valid for 12 months from the date of issue and can be used for any future travel on ACME Bharat Airlines.

## 4. Airline-Initiated Cancellations
In the event that ACME Bharat Airlines cancels a scheduled flight due to operational issues, technical difficulties, or scheduling changes, passengers will be provided with the following options:
* A full refund of the unused ticket value, returned to the original form of payment.
* Free rebooking on the next available ACME Bharat Airlines flight to the scheduled destination, with any fare differences and change fees waived.

## 5. Weather and Force Majeure Cancellations
In cases where flights are cancelled due to adverse weather conditions, natural disasters, or other factors beyond the control of the airline:
* ACME Bharat Airlines will provide no monetary compensation (such as hotel accommodation or meals).
* However, affected passengers are eligible for either free rebooking on the next available flight or a full refund of their ticket value.

## 6. Requesting a Refund
To request a refund under any of the provisions listed above, passengers should visit the "Manage Booking" section on the ACME Bharat Airlines website or contact our customer support center. For tickets purchased through third-party travel agencies, passengers must contact their travel agent directly to initiate the refund process.
