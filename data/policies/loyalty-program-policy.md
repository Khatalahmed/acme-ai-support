# ACME Bharat Airlines: AcmeMiles Loyalty Program Policy

## 1. Program Overview
The AcmeMiles Loyalty Program is the official frequent flyer program of ACME Bharat Airlines. Designed to reward our valued passengers for their continued patronage, the program allows registered members to accumulate AcmeMiles through qualifying air travel and redeem them for award flights, cabin upgrades, and other exclusive privileges. Membership is voluntary and subject to the terms, conditions, and guidelines outlined in this official policy document.

## 2. Earning AcmeMiles
Members earn AcmeMiles on all eligible scheduled commercial flights operated by ACME Bharat Airlines. The accumulation of miles is calculated based on the net monetary value of the ticket purchase.
* Members shall earn 5 AcmeMiles per Rs 100 spent on the base fare of an eligible ticket.
* The base fare calculation strictly excludes any government-imposed taxes, airport development fees, passenger service charges, fuel surcharges, and GST.
* Miles are automatically credited to the member's profile within 48 hours of flight completion, provided the membership number was entered at the time of booking.

## 3. Tier Levels and Membership Status
The AcmeMiles program features three elite tier levels designed to recognize and reward our most frequent travelers. Tier qualification is determined by the total number of qualifying miles accumulated within a single calendar year (January 1 to December 31).

* **Silver Tier**: Attained upon earning 20,000 miles within a single calendar year.
* **Gold Tier**: Attained upon earning 50,000 miles within a single calendar year.
* **Platinum Tier**: Attained upon earning 100,000 miles within a single calendar year.

Once a member achieves an elite tier, the status remains valid for the remainder of the current calendar year plus the entirety of the subsequent calendar year. Elite benefits include priority check-in, additional baggage allowances, and complimentary lounge access.

## 4. Mileage Expiration
To preserve accumulated miles, members must maintain active participation in the AcmeMiles program. 
* All accumulated miles shall expire after 24 months of account inactivity.
* Account inactivity is defined as any continuous 24-month period during which a member does not record any qualifying transaction, such as earning miles through a flight or redeeming miles for an eligible reward.
* Expired miles are permanently forfeited and cannot be reinstated, transferred, or exchanged for monetary compensation.

## 5. Retroactive Mileage Claims
In the event that eligible miles are not automatically credited to a member's account following a flight, a retroactive claim may be initiated.
* Any missing miles must be claimed within 90 days of the date of travel.
* Members must submit a formal claim request accompanied by a copy of their physical or digital boarding pass, along with the ticket receipt.
* Claims submitted without a valid boarding pass, or those initiated after the 90-day window has elapsed, will be systematically rejected.

## 6. Policy Amendments and Governance
ACME Bharat Airlines reserves the right to modify, amend, or terminate the AcmeMiles Loyalty Program rules, tier benefits, and earning ratios at its sole discretion. Any changes to the policy will be communicated to members through official channels.
