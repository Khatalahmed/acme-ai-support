# ACME Bharat Airlines: Flight Delay Compensation Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This document outlines the official policy of ACME Bharat Airlines regarding passenger compensation, care, and assistance in the event of flight delays. ACME Bharat Airlines is committed to providing reliable, safe, and punctual air travel. However, when flight disruptions occur, we aim to minimize passenger inconvenience through fair and transparent compensation standards. This policy applies to all ticketed passengers traveling on flights operated by ACME Bharat Airlines.

## 2. Delay Compensation Tiers
In the event of a flight delay, ACME Bharat Airlines will provide care and compensation based on the total duration of the delay. The delay duration is calculated from the scheduled time of departure. 

*   **Delay of 2-4 hours:**
    Passengers experiencing a flight departure delay of 2-4 hours will be provided with complimentary meal vouchers worth Rs 400. These vouchers can be redeemed at designated food and beverage outlets within the departure airport terminal.

*   **Delay of 4-6 hours:**
    For flights delayed by 4-6 hours, passengers are entitled to choose one of the following options:
    *   A Rs 3,000 travel voucher valid for future travel with ACME Bharat Airlines, OR
    *   Free rebooking onto the next available ACME Bharat Airlines flight to the same destination, subject to seat availability, with all modification fees waived.

*   **Delay over 6 hours:**
    For flights delayed by over 6 hours, passengers are entitled to choose one of the following options:
    *   A full refund of the ticket price for the affected flight sector, OR
    *   Free rebooking onto an alternative ACME Bharat Airlines flight, plus a Rs 5,000 travel voucher valid for future travel.

## 3. Overnight Delays
In the event of an overnight delay, where the rescheduled departure time requires an unscheduled overnight stay, complimentary hotel accommodation will be provided by the airline. ACME Bharat Airlines will also arrange and cover the cost of ground transportation between the airport and the hotel.

## 4. Policy Exclusions
ACME Bharat Airlines strives to maintain operational punctuality. However, certain circumstances are entirely beyond the airline's control. Under this policy, there is no compensation for delays caused by weather or air traffic control. Passengers affected by delays resulting from these specific external factors will not be eligible for travel vouchers, monetary refunds, or meal coupons under this policy.

## 5. Processing Claims and Payouts
Eligible passengers can file a compensation claim at the airport service desk or online through the official ACME Bharat Airlines claims portal. All approved compensation payouts are processed within 7 business days from the date of claim verification and approval. Refund amounts will be credited back to the original mode of payment used during ticket purchase.
